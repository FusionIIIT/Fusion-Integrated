[Title] LMS (Course Management)
Workflow Specifications — Version 1.0
SYNCHRONIZED DRAFT — supported paths only
[Heading 1] 1. Scope and authority
Workflows are derived from the synchronized state-changing UCs and applicable BRs. The State-Transition Table (STT) is authoritative; diagrams are corresponding visual views. Informational/view UCs remain outside workflows. Event-triggered UC-LMS-015 is used as a System-performed workflow step only when it changes content from Scheduled to Published/Available.
[Heading 1] 2. Workflow derivation decisions
[List Bullet] A Basis Workflow is a complete finite valid progression from a meaningful start/context to a meaningful business outcome; prefixes or isolated actions are not promoted to BWs.
[List Bullet] A Composite Workflow combines related Basis Workflows only where the integrated view improves understanding.
[List Bullet] Business Rules constrain transitions through guards/conditions and are not workflow activities.
[List Bullet] System may appear in the STT Actor column only for a meaningful automatic state-changing transition. A time event is a trigger/guard, not an actor.
[List Bullet] Late submission and resubmission paths are not modeled because their policies are explicitly unresolved in PS v1.0.
[List Bullet] Post-release grade-change lifecycle is not modeled beyond the audit obligation because authority/effect are unresolved.
[List Bullet] No complete all-inclusive workflow is forced; assignment publication/submission and grading/release remain separate composites.
