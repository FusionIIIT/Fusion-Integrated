Behavioral preservation. The v1.3 state-transition behavior remains valid and is preserved. This version adds the required CFR/AFR, UC, and BR traceability fields. HM-UC-025 and HM-UC-034 are event-triggered services but are intentionally not added as workflow transitions because they are isolated record/notification services rather than connected business-progression states. The STT remains authoritative.
