Workflow Specifications

Gymkhana Module

Status: SYNCHRONIZED v1.1

Direct-final workflow review applies the revised workflow definition: actor count is not an eligibility criterion. A connected progression of multiple state-changing UCs may therefore be a Workflow even when the same actor performs successive transitions. BW-GYM-01 and BW-GYM-02 remain valid. BW-GYM-03 is added for the Student Admin club-establishment lifecycle. No Composite Workflow is forced because the three Basis Workflows serve distinct business purposes and do not form a useful common progression.

Workflow Exclusions / Non-Workflow Functions

Book Club Session and Book Club Event are state-changing but isolated booking services; no connected multi-UC progression is specified for either in the accepted baseline.

Change Club Leadership is a single state-changing actor service; no additional lifecycle stage is specified.

Request Club Budget is a single submission/tracking service because online budget approval/rejection is explicitly out of scope.

Submit Club Event Report is a state-changing submission, but the subsequent Faculty Councillor review is information access only and has no accepted persistent review state; a report-review workflow is therefore not invented.

Notifications and validation rules constrain or support UCs but are not standalone Workflow activities.
