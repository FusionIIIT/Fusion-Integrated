Employee Leave Management (ELM) Requirements Summary

1. **Overview**
   - ELM is an institute ERP used for administrative, academic, student, and related services.
   - Supports leave-related activities for institute employees, including CL, RH, SCL, EL, COL, and VL.

2. **Leave Categories and Station Leave**
   - CL: 8 days/year, first-half and second-half supported, half-day consumes 0.5 day.
   - RH: 2 days/year, must correspond to an applicable Restricted Holiday.
   - SCL: up to 15 days/year for special purposes.
   - EL: 30 days/year for non-faculty employees, carries forward.
   - VL: 60 days/year associated with vacation periods, converts to EL at 2:1 ratio.
   - COL: 20 days/year, medical/balance-controlled leave, carries forward.

3. **Entitlement, Applicability, and Carry-Forward**
   - Leave entitlements are based on employee category and organizational unit.
   - Leave-day counting is continuous across approved leave intervals.
   - Unused leave does not carry forward.

4. **Applying for Leave and Validation**
   - Employees submit one leave application with category, requested period, purpose, and other required information.
   - Validation checks eligibility, available entitlement, leave-counting conditions, and impermissible overlap.

5. **Substitute / Responsibility Coverage**
   - When required, employees nominate substitutes for their absence.
   - Substitutes must be eligible and available during the responsibility period.
   - Substitute consent is required before approval routing continues.

6. **Review, Sanction, and Decision Communication**
   - CL and RH requests are reviewed by the applicant's Unit Head.
   - SCL, EL, COL, and VL requests are reviewed by the Unit Head and then higher-sanction authorities.
   - Final decisions are recorded, and employees are notified. Leave records are updated on final approval.

7. **Pending Request, Withdrawal, and Approved-Leave Cancellation**
   - Pending requests are not modified in place. Employees can withdraw requests before final approval.
   - Approved leave can be canceled before it starts, with the option to submit a new application if needed.

8. **Extension**
   - Employees can request additional leave time while on ongoing EL, COL, or VL.
   - Extensions are validated and follow the same approval process as new requests.

9. **Resumption**
   - Employees can declare actual return to duty after EL, COL, or VL.
   - Resumption is reviewed by the applicable Sanctioning Authority, adjusting leave balances accordingly.

10. **Inquiry and Employee-Accessible Records**
    - Employees can view their current leave balances and request history.
    - Printable/exportable representations of leave applications are available.

11. **Administrative Functions and Calendars**
    - Authorized administrative roles maintain leave-policy parameters, hierarchy configuration, and leave records.
    - Administrative roles create, revise, and publish the holiday/RH calendar.

12. **Event-Triggered Year-End Processing**
    - Year-end processing applies category outcomes and records transactions.
    - Year-end boundary is an event trigger, not an actor.

13. **SLA**
    - Configurable reminder and escalation thresholds for actionable workflow tasks.
    - Notifications and escalations are logged based on task status.

14. **Scope Boundary**
    - ELM limits leave categories to CL, RH, SCL, EL, COL, VL, and Station Leave.
    - Other leave types and low-level design/implementation details are outside this requirements baseline.

This summary provides a concise overview of the key functionalities and processes in the ELM system, which can be used as a reference for developers working on the implementation.
