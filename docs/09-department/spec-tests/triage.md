### Accepted dependency evidence

- **Profiles (17 cases).** BR-DEPT-019 in the Business Rule Specifications says, “The exact field list and privacy policy are external/not specified.” CFR-DEPT-010 still requires “role- and relationship-specific profile visibility” and AFR-DEPT-024 requires Dept_Admin to manage profile records within the department. The requirement registry says “Specific CRUD sub-actions are not invented.” The accepted profile contract must supply a field policy, allowed writes, and authoritative enrollment relationships before Department can verify these cases.
- **IAM assignments and Headship (22 cases).** BR-DEPT-021 in the Business Rule Specifications says, “Only the central Institute Admin or equivalent central authority may assign or reassign the HOD and Dept_Admin roles for a department; the active HOD assignment is authoritative for the department Headship identity displayed by this module.” BR-DEPT-022 through BR-DEPT-024 require single active holders, effective intervals, and immediate access handoff. The current IAM `IamUserDesignation` model only enforces uniqueness by user and designation, and its `assign_role` command grants or revokes a single designation. The central assignment contract and Headship projection still need to provide those guarantees before integration verification.
