Business Rule Specifications — Final Synchronized v1.4
Fusion ERP • Employee Leave Management • Synchronized baseline v1.4
Rule disciplineBusiness Rules externalize material policy, eligibility, authorization, calculation, routing, lifecycle, and threshold constraints. They constrain actor-triggered or event-triggered Use Cases and workflow transitions; they are not workflow activities. BR-EL-021 and BR-EL-026 remain retired identifiers for traceability. BR-EL-030 uses current configured policy/hierarchy values; holiday/RH calendar publication semantics remain under BR-EL-031.

Reminder and escalation thresholds for SLA-controlled actionable workflow tasks shall be configurable by an authorized administrative role and shall govern EL-UC-021 event-triggered SLA processing.
Applies To
SLA configuration
Condition / Trigger
Authorized threshold change
Required Outcome / Constraint
Store valid thresholds and use them in EL-UC-021.
Related CFRs
CFR-EL-029
Related Use Cases
EL-UC-018; EL-UC-021
Related Workflow(s)
N/A
Status / Revision Note
New explicit configuration rule.
