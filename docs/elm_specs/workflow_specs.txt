Employee Leave Management (ELM)
Workflow Specifications — Final Synchronized v1.6
Corrected Basis-Workflow completeness • 17 complete Basis Workflows • 4 Composite Workflows • authoritative STTs
1. Revision Scope and Authority
Version 1.6 corrects a Basis-Workflow derivation defect in v1.5: reusable substitute-consent/recovery prefixes were incorrectly counted as Basis Workflows, leaving some complete valid UC sequences (for example EL-UC-001 → EL-UC-002 → EL-UC-003 for substitute-required CL/RH) without a corresponding Basis Workflow. No PS, CFR/AFR, UC, or BR semantics are changed.
The Composite Workflows remain semantically unchanged. Their constituent Basis-Workflow references are updated to the corrected complete-path set. The State-Transition Table (STT) remains authoritative and each diagram is a corresponding visual view.
2. Workflow Derivation Decisions
Derive workflows only from validated state-changing UCs and applicable BRs.
A Basis Workflow shall be a complete finite valid progression from a meaningful process start/context to a meaningful business outcome; a reusable prefix, suffix, or recovery fragment is not by itself a Basis Workflow unless it independently constitutes such a progression.
A sequence such as Apply → Substitute Response → Unit Head Review must therefore appear as a complete Basis Workflow when it is a valid business path.
When terminal outcomes differ only in the outcome of the same final decision UC and all preceding steps are identical, they may be represented as alternative terminal rows in one Basis Workflow to avoid duplicate path specifications.
A purely configuration-controlled optional/bypass step (for example the Establishment routing step) may remain within the same Basis Workflow when it does not create a different business outcome.
For a meaningful loop, represent zero traversal and exactly one traversal; do not enumerate further equivalent repetitions.
Composite Workflows integrate complete Basis Workflows and may additionally represent cross-cutting transitions such as pending withdrawal and routing-changing SLA escalation.
ELM is not its own UC actor. System may appear in an STT only as the performer of a meaningful automatic transition; time/external events remain triggers/guards.
Pending requests are not modified in place. Change before final approval is handled through withdrawal and, if needed, a fresh application.
Year-end processing and reminder/notification-only SLA handling remain event-triggered UC+BR behavior, not standalone workflows.
The STT is authoritative; diagrams must correspond to it exactly.
