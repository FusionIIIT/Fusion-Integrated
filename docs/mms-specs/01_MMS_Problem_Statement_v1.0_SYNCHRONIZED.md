# MMS Reviewed and Frozen Problem Statement v1.0

## 3.1 Overview

The Mess Management System (MMS) digitizes and supports operational activities related to student mess services in the institute. The module is used by authorized institute employees and students for mess-service administration, student participation, operational decision making, supervision, and communication.

MMS supports role assignment for mess roles, student registration and deregistration, menu publication, monthly bill generation and publication, rebate request handling, vacation food continuity surveys, special food requests, announcements, feedback recording, reporting, and system-generated email notifications for specified MMS events.

The system has four actor roles:

Mess Admin — assigns and reassigns active Mess Caretaker and Mess Warden roles for specified durations.

Student — consumes mess services and submits or views mess-related requests, responses, bills, menus, announcements, and feedback.

Mess Caretaker — manages day-to-day mess operations supported by MMS.

Mess Warden — supervises mess operations through oversight, report viewing, announcements, and feedback review without performing operational approvals.

MMS is limited to mess-management functions. Employee identity records, payroll, institutional accounting, payment collection, procurement, inventory management, and email infrastructure are outside MMS scope unless explicitly represented as data consumed by MMS or as system-generated email notifications triggered by MMS events.

## 3.2 Actors and Responsibilities

### 3.2.1 Mess Admin

Assigns the Mess Caretaker role and Mess Warden role to specific institute employees for specified durations.

Reassigns these roles when required, including mid-cycle reassignment.

Ensures that exactly one active Mess Caretaker and exactly one active Mess Warden exist at a time in MMS.

Has no access to operational mess data, student requests, billing details, rebate details, special meal requests, vacation responses, or feedback entries.

### 3.2.2 Student

Submits mess registration and deregistration requests.

Views weekly mess menus, monthly bills, and outstanding balances.

Submits rebate requests, special food requests, and vacation food continuity responses.

Views announcements relevant to mess services.

Provides text feedback on mess services.

Tracks the status and history of submitted MMS requests.

### 3.2.3 Mess Caretaker

Opens registration windows for mess cycles.

Reviews and approves or rejects student registration and deregistration requests.

Publishes and updates weekly mess menus.

Reviews, finalizes, and publishes monthly mess bills generated by MMS.

Reviews and approves or rejects rebate requests and may modify rebate dates before approval where permitted by the stated mess policy.

Creates and manages vacation food continuity surveys and views student response summaries.

Reviews and approves or rejects special food requests.

Publishes announcements where the Caretaker is the announcement owner.

Reviews student feedback entries.

Generates operational reports such as registration trends, special meal logs, and billing summaries.

### 3.2.4 Mess Warden

Reviews feedback and announcements for supervisory oversight.

Views and searches supervisory reports such as registered student lists, rebate summaries, and vacation survey response summaries.

Publishes announcements where the Warden is the announcement owner.

May view operational requests for oversight but does not approve, reject, register, deregister, or decide operational student requests.

## 3.3 Functional Scope

### 3.3.1 Registration Management

The Mess Caretaker opens a registration window at the start of each mess cycle, such as a semester or other designated period.

Students may submit registration requests only while the registration window is open.

Students may submit deregistration requests at any time.

The Mess Caretaker reviews each registration or deregistration request and approves or rejects it.

A deregistration approval is recorded when the Caretaker decides the request; the service effect of approved deregistration starts from the next calendar day.

The Mess Warden may view registration and deregistration requests for oversight but does not act on them.

MMS records request history, maintains request status, and sends system-generated email notifications to the student for request submission and approval or rejection decisions.

### 3.3.2 Menu Management

MMS stores the weekly mess menu in a day-wise and meal-wise structure covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Students, Mess Caretaker, Mess Warden, and Mess Admin may view the weekly menu according to their access rights; operational menu maintenance is owned by the Mess Caretaker.

The Mess Caretaker may add, delete, or modify menu items.

Menu updates trigger system-generated email notifications to students.

### 3.3.3 Billing Management

MMS generates monthly mess bills using the calendar-month billing cycle.

Bill computation includes daily-rate charges for served days, charges for approved special meals or add-ons, and deductions for approved rebates.

The Mess Caretaker reviews and finalizes generated bills before publishing them.

Students may view their monthly bills and outstanding balances.

MMS supports bill generation, finalization, publication, and viewing. Payment collection, accounting settlement, and payroll/account integration are outside MMS scope unless handled by another Fusion module.

### 3.3.4 Feedback Management

Students may submit text feedback related to mess services.

The Mess Caretaker and Mess Warden may view feedback entries for operational review, supervisory oversight, record keeping, and analysis.

MMS stores feedback entries with relevant record information. The problem statement does not define a formal feedback resolution workflow.

### 3.3.5 Rebate Management

Students may apply for a mess rebate by specifying a reason and a start date and end date.

Allowed reason categories are medical, institute leave, family function, and other. If “other” is selected, an explanation is mandatory.

The minimum rebate duration is 3 days.

A rebate request must be submitted at least 1 calendar day before the rebate start date.

Overlapping rebate requests for the same student are not allowed.

The Mess Caretaker may approve or reject a rebate request.

The Mess Caretaker may modify rebate dates before approval where permitted by the stated mess policy; the approved dates become the dates used by MMS for billing adjustment.

Students may track rebate request status as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

Approved rebates automatically adjust the corresponding monthly mess bill.

### 3.3.6 Vacation Food Continuity Management

The Mess Caretaker creates a vacation food continuity survey for an upcoming institute vacation.

Only currently registered students receive the survey.

Students respond Yes or No to indicate whether they require mess food continuity during the vacation period.

The Mess Caretaker sets a response deadline for each survey.

For students who do not respond by the deadline, MMS records the response as No.

The Mess Caretaker reviews the response summary for operational planning.

### 3.3.7 Special Food Arrangement

Students may request special food due to illness or personal events at least 1 calendar day before the requested date.

The Mess Caretaker may approve or reject the request within the stipulated decision period. The source statement indicates 12 hours as the intended decision period; the exact policy value should be treated as institution-defined for later detailed specification.

If the request is not decided within the stipulated decision period, MMS treats the request as rejected by default.

MMS records request history and sends system-generated email notifications to the student for request submission and decision outcomes.

Approved special food requests are chargeable and are added to the student’s monthly bill.

MMS tracks the request and fulfillment status. The allowed request states are Requested, Approved, Rejected, Served, and Cancelled. Served and Cancelled are applicable after approval where relevant.

### 3.3.8 Announcements

The Mess Caretaker or Mess Warden may publish mess-related announcements.

Students receive system-generated email notifications for new announcements.

MMS maintains announcement history relevant to mess services.

All MMS actors may view announcement history according to their access rights.

### 3.3.9 Reports

The Mess Warden can view and search registered student lists, rebate summaries, and vacation survey response summaries.

The Mess Caretaker may generate operational reports such as registration trends, special meal logs, and billing summaries.

Reports are limited to MMS operational and supervisory information. External financial, payroll, procurement, or inventory reports are outside MMS scope.

### 3.3.10 Notifications

MMS sends system-generated email notifications for the following MMS events:

Registration and deregistration request submission.

Approval or rejection of registration and deregistration requests.

Rebate request submission and decision.

Special food request submission, decision, and fulfillment-related updates where applicable.

Vacation food continuity survey creation and reminders.

Menu updates.

Monthly bill publication.

New announcements.

MMS is responsible for triggering system-generated email notifications for defined events. The external email delivery infrastructure is outside MMS scope.

### 3.3.11 Role Management

The Mess Admin assigns and reassigns the Mess Caretaker role and Mess Warden role to specific institute employees for specified durations.

Roles are time-bound.

Mid-cycle reassignment is allowed and takes effect immediately in MMS from the recorded reassignment time.

Pending operational requests become visible to and actionable by the newly assigned active Mess Caretaker from the effective reassignment time.

Exactly one active Mess Caretaker and exactly one active Mess Warden exist at a time.

Mess Admin is limited to role assignment and reassignment and has no access to operational mess data.

## 3.4 Scope Boundary Clarifications

MMS does not own institute employee master data; it uses employee identities needed for Mess Admin, Mess Caretaker, and Mess Warden role assignment.

MMS does not perform payment collection, accounting settlement, payroll processing, procurement, stock management, or inventory management.

MMS does not define institutional mess policies such as rates, vacation periods, eligibility policy, or approval authority beyond the behavior explicitly stated in this problem statement.

MMS does not treat the system itself as a primary actor. System behavior is represented as validation, record maintenance, status changes, billing adjustment, history logging, report generation, and system-generated email notifications.
