# Fusion ERP HR-EIS Specification Reference\n\n\n# 01_HR_Problem_Statement_v1.2_SYNCHRONIZED\n\nFusion ERP — Human Resource Module (HR-EIS)\n\nSynchronization note. The source PS was found semantically sound and is preserved without functional expansion. The synchronized UC/BR/WF/RTM package is constrained to the behavior stated below; unsupported legacy workflow behavior was removed rather than added to this PS.\n\n1. Overview\n\nThe Human Resource - Employee Information System (HR-EIS) is a core administrative module of Fusion ERP deployed at IIITDM Jabalpur. The module digitizes, centralizes, and governs employee-centric information and HR service workflows for faculty and staff of the institute.\n\nHR-EIS provides a single authoritative system for managing employee service records, leave management, yearly appraisals, financial entitlement workflows for Leave Travel Concession (LTC) and Cumulative Professional Development Allowance (CPDA), approvals, audit trails, and HR-related integration records with payroll and finance systems.\n\nThe module replaces fragmented manual processes with standardized, role-driven, traceable digital workflows to support accuracy, transparency, and policy-compliant HR processing.\n\nHR-EIS operates under Fusion role-based access control (RBAC). Platform-wide authentication, user-account administration, and RBAC infrastructure are outside HR-EIS except where HR-EIS consumes assigned roles or records HR-specific role/access effects.\n\nHR-EIS records, validates, routes, tracks, notifies, archives, and audits HR-related information. External payroll, finance, email, dashboard, and other Fusion modules retain ownership of their own domain-specific execution unless HR-EIS explicitly records or synchronizes related status, claim, payment, identity, role, or notification information.\n\n2. Actors\n\nThe HR-EIS module is used by the following actors:\n\n2.1 Employee (Faculty / Staff)\n\nAn Employee is an institute faculty or staff member who uses HR-EIS to initiate and track HR-related service requests and to view personal HR information.\n\nSubmits HR-related requests.\n\nViews personal service records in read-only mode.\n\nTracks request statuses.\n\nReceives HR-EIS notifications.\n\n2.2 Approving Authorities\n\nApproving Authorities are institutional roles responsible for reviewing and deciding HR requests according to employee category and request type.\n\nHead of Department (HOD): performs department-level approvals where applicable.\n\nRegistrar: performs approvals for non-teaching staff where applicable.\n\nDirector: performs approvals for faculty-level or final authority cases where applicable.\n\nApproval authority is determined by employee category and request type. The exact approval matrix is an institutional policy/configuration outside this problem statement unless represented through HR-EIS workflow configuration.\n\n2.3 HR Administrator\n\nThe HR Administrator is an institutional HR official responsible for HR-EIS operational administration and HR record verification.\n\nManages employee master and service records.\n\nVerifies HR requests, documentation, and eligibility based on configured rules.\n\nConfigures HR-EIS workflow settings according to institutional policy.\n\nAssigns appraisal reviewers.\n\nMonitors HR compliance and audit records.\n\n2.4 Accountant\n\nThe Accountant is a finance authority who handles the HR-EIS-side processing of approved financial entitlement claims.\n\nProcesses approved LTC and CPDA claims in HR-EIS for financial settlement.\n\nVerifies financial correctness of approved claims according to applicable configured rules.\n\nRecords payment or disbursement status linked to payroll or finance systems.\n\nActual payroll, finance-accounting, or bank-disbursement execution remains outside HR-EIS.\n\n3. Functional Scope\n\n3.1 Employee Information and Service Records\n\nHR-EIS maintains a centralized service record for each employee.\n\nService records include appointment details, department, designation, service history, leave balances, and retirement status.\n\nOnly HR Administrators can create or modify employee service records in HR-EIS.\n\nEmployees can view their own service records in read-only mode.\n\n3.2 Leave Management\n\nEmployees may apply for Casual, Restricted, Medical, Earned, Vacation, and Sabbatical leave.\n\nEach leave request must specify leave type, duration, and justification.\n\nHR-EIS validates leave balance and eligibility before accepting leave submission.\n\nLeave requests follow the predefined approval hierarchy applicable to employee category and request type.\n\nApproved leave updates the employee leave balance automatically.\n\nAll leave transactions are permanently logged.\n\n3.3 Yearly Appraisal Management\n\nEmployees submit yearly appraisal forms within configured submission windows.\n\nHR Administrator assigns reviewers for appraisal evaluation.\n\nAppraisal workflows pass through designated authorities for review and finalization.\n\nFinalized appraisals are archived in the employee service record.\n\nAppraisal records are immutable after closure.\n\n3.4 Leave Travel Concession (LTC) Management\n\nEmployees may submit LTC advance or reimbursement requests using prescribed forms.\n\nHR Administrator verifies LTC documentation and eligibility.\n\nVerified LTC requests move through the applicable approval hierarchy.\n\nAccountant processes approved LTC claims in HR-EIS for financial settlement.\n\nPayment status is recorded in HR-EIS and linked to payroll or finance systems.\n\nRejected or returned LTC requests retain full audit history.\n\n3.5 Cumulative Professional Development Allowance (CPDA) Management\n\nEmployees may apply for CPDA advances or reimbursements.\n\nCPDA requests must include purpose, amount, and supporting documents.\n\nHR Administrator verifies CPDA claims before approval routing.\n\nAccountant processes approved CPDA claims in HR-EIS.\n\nDisbursement details are recorded in HR-EIS and remain auditable.\n\n3.6 Approval Workflow Management\n\nAll HR-EIS requests follow rule-based workflows.\n\nRequest decisions may include Approved, Rejected, or Forwarded to next authority.\n\nAuthority roles are enforced through RBAC and workflow configuration.\n\nNo authority can bypass or alter the configured workflow order within HR-EIS.\n\n3.7 Notifications\n\nHR-EIS generates system notifications for request submission.\n\nHR-EIS generates system notifications for approval, rejection, or forwarding.\n\nHR-EIS generates pending action reminders.\n\nHR-EIS generates policy or deadline update notifications where such policy/deadline information is configured or represented in HR-EIS.\n\nNotifications are delivered through the Fusion dashboard and official email. Dashboard and email delivery infrastructure remain outside HR-EIS.\n\n3.8 Audit and Compliance\n\nEvery HR transaction is logged with actor identity, timestamp, and action performed.\n\nAudit logs are immutable.\n\nAudit logs are accessible only to authorized roles.\n\nHR-EIS supports compliance with institutional HR and financial policies by enforcing configured validations, workflow routing, logging, and access restrictions.\n\n3.9 Integration\n\nHR-EIS records and synchronizes payment or disbursement status with payroll systems where applicable.\n\nHR-EIS records and synchronizes accounting-related claim information with finance systems where applicable.\n\nHR-EIS exchanges identity and role-consistency information with other Fusion modules where applicable.\n\nData synchronization is automatic and real-time where configured. External systems retain ownership of their respective payroll, finance, identity, and module-specific execution semantics.\n\n4. Role Management and Access Control\n\nFusion enforces RBAC for HR-EIS functions.\n\nA user may hold multiple roles simultaneously.\n\nRole assignment and reassignment are controlled by institutional authorities outside routine HR request processing.\n\nAccess to sensitive HR data is restricted to authorized roles.\n\nEmployees can access only their own HR data unless they also hold another authorized role.\n\n5. Non-Functional Constraints\n\nHR-EIS must support data accuracy, security, and consistency for employee service records and HR workflows.\n\nHR-EIS workflows must be traceable and auditable.\n\nHR-EIS must prevent unauthorized access or data modification through RBAC and workflow controls.\n\nDowntime or partial updates must not corrupt employee service records.\n\n6. Exclusions and Scope Boundaries\n\nHR-EIS does not modify payroll rules or salary structures.\n\nHR-EIS does not execute payroll, accounting, bank transfer, or finance-system disbursement operations.\n\nHR-EIS does not allow manual bypass of configured approval workflows.\n\nHR-EIS does not permit Employees to alter official service records.\n\nPlatform-level authentication, account creation, and Fusion-wide RBAC infrastructure are outside HR-EIS unless represented as HR-EIS role/access effects.\n\nExternal institutional HR policies, financial policies, leave rules, LTC/CPDA rules, and appraisal rules are outside HR-EIS unless HR-EIS explicitly validates, restricts, routes, records, notifies, audits, or reports based on them.\n\nThe System is not treated as a regular primary actor in use cases derived later from this problem statement. System behavior appears as internal validation, workflow routing, balance update, notification, archival, audit logging, maintained data, integration event, or postcondition behavior.\n\nEnd of synchronized Problem Statement.\n\n\n## Table 1\n\nModule | Human Resource — Employee Information System (HR-EIS)\n\nArtifact | Problem Statement\n\nVersion | v1.2_SYNCHRONIZED\n\nDate | 2026-08-22\n\nGoverning baseline | Source HR Problem Statement v1.2 reviewed against the synchronized package\n\nStatus | SYNCHRONIZED\n\n\n# 02_HR_Requirement_Registry_CFR_AFR_v1.3_FINAL\n\nHR-EIS Requirement Registry — Coherent / Consolidated Requirements (CFR) and Atomic Requirements (AFR)\n\nRegistry discipline: the v1.2 HR-FR registry is preserved as historical continuity evidence.
