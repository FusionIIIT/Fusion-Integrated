Other Academic Procedures (OAP) • Final Synchronized Baseline
This registry replaces the single-level FR registry only for final engineering use. The 51 accepted atomic obligations are preserved in meaning as AFRs and grouped into 22 coherent CFRs. No requirement has been added merely to make the hierarchy symmetrical. Historical FR-OAP-003, FR-OAP-050 and FR-OAP-051 are retained only as retired continuity evidence because disciplinary/student-issue handling is outside the accepted OAP scope.
