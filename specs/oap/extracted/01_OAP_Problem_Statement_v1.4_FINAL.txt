OTHER ACADEMIC PROCEDURES (OAP) — PROBLEM STATEMENT
Other Academic Procedures (OAP) • Final Synchronized Baseline
1. Overview
The Other Academic Procedures (OAP) module supports a set of academic administrative procedures that do not belong to the core registration/examination flows. It provides role-controlled handling of student leave, bonafide certificate requests, no-dues clearance, PG assistantship claims, graduate seminar scheduling, and departmental supervisor/TA assignment recording.
The module is intended to make these procedures visible and traceable to the participating users while providing role-based access control, system-generated alerts, email notifications, and reminders for pending actions.

3. Functional Scope
3.1 Leave Management
Students submit leave applications with or without supporting documents.
The appropriate HoD reviews each submitted leave application and approves or rejects it.
Students can track current leave status and available request history.
The system notifies students of submission/decision events as required by the common notification scope.
3.2 Bonafide Certificate Management
Students submit bonafide certificate requests and can monitor their request status.
The Academic Administrator verifies each request and approves or rejects it.
Approved bonafide certificates are made available to the requesting student for direct download.
The system notifies the student of the decision and certificate availability as applicable.
3.3 No-Dues Clearance Management
Students initiate no-dues clearance.
The system routes every no-dues request in parallel to all seven mandatory clearance domains: Library, Hostel, Mess, the student’s respective Department, Lab, Academic Section, and Student Section.
Each of the seven authorities records clearance status only for its own domain; all seven authority statuses are maintained separately.
The Academic Administrator reviews/consolidates the completed seven-authority verification set and finalizes no-dues clearance only when all seven required clearances are recorded as clear.
Students can track per-authority progress and download the finalized no-dues certificate.
3.4 Assistantship Claim Management (PG Students)
PG students submit assistantship claims.
Both the assigned Thesis Supervisor and the assigned TA Supervisor verify the claim. The two roles may be held by the same faculty member or by different faculty members; both role-verifications are required.
The Department Administrator reviews a claim only after both Thesis Supervisor and TA Supervisor verifications are complete, and then forwards it to HoD.
The HoD approves or rejects the claim.
The Academic Administrator processes stipend disbursement for a HoD-approved claim.
PG students can track claim status across the stages.
3.5 Supervisor and TA Assignment Recording
The Department Administrator records thesis-supervisor assignments based on departmental decisions.
The Department Administrator records TA assignments based on departmental decisions, associating a PG student as Teaching Assistant with a course/subject, a faculty member, or both, as specified by the departmental decision.
For assistantship processing, Thesis Supervisor and TA Supervisor are distinct verification roles even when the same faculty member holds both roles. A TA Supervisor must be identifiable before an assistantship claim can progress through dual-supervisor verification.
3.6 Graduate Seminar Management (PG Students)
PG students request graduate seminar scheduling.
The Department Administrator schedules and logs graduate seminars.
PG students and faculty can view graduate seminar schedules.
The system maintains seminar scheduling history. No separate seminar approval stage is assumed because none is specified.
3.7 Notifications and Reminders
The system issues system-generated alerts and email notifications for request submission, approval/rejection, completion of no-dues verification, and availability of downloadable certificates.
The system issues reminders for pending actions. Exact reminder schedules/thresholds are not specified by the accepted source.
3.8 Security and Role-Based Access
Access to OAP functions is governed by role-based access control.
Users may access only information/functions relevant to their assigned roles.
4. Scope Exclusions / Not Assumed
No withdrawal/cancellation/reschedule functionality is part of the accepted baseline unless separately approved.
No fixed reminder times, escalation thresholds, SMS notification channel, certificate validity period, digital-signature rule, file-size/type rule, seminar conflict rule, or claim duplicate rule is assumed.
Disciplinary and student-issue reporting, routing, access-policy handling, and downstream counselor/disciplinary-committee processing are outside the OAP module scope for this baseline.
No specific finance/payroll integration for stipend disbursement is assumed.

Actor / Role / System Responsibility | Responsibilities
Student (UG/PG) | Submit and track leave applications; request, monitor, and download approved bonafide certificates; initiate and track no-dues clearance; download finalized no-dues certificates. PG students additionally submit/track assistantship claims and request graduate seminar scheduling.
Faculty Member | View graduate seminar schedules. A faculty member may additionally hold the Thesis Supervisor role, the TA Supervisor role, or both when assigned.
Thesis Supervisor | Verify assistantship claims for PG students for whom the faculty member is the assigned Thesis Supervisor.
TA Supervisor | Verify assistantship claims for PG students for whom the faculty member is the assigned TA Supervisor.
Head of Department (HoD) | Review and approve/reject student leave applications; approve/reject assistantship claims after both supervisor-role verifications and Department Administrator review.
Academic Administrator | Verify and decide bonafide requests; review/consolidate and finalize no-dues clearance after all seven mandatory clearances; process stipend disbursement for HoD-approved assistantship claims.
Department Administrator | Schedule and log graduate seminars; record thesis-supervisor assignments and TA assignments based on departmental decisions; review assistantship claims after both required supervisor verifications and forward them to HoD.
No-Dues Clearance Authorities | Librarian verifies Library clearance; Hostel Warden verifies Hostel clearance; Mess In-charge verifies Mess clearance; an authorized respective-Department verifier records Department clearance; Lab Supervisor verifies Lab clearance; an authorized Academic Section verifier records Academic Section clearance; an authorized Student Section verifier records Student Section clearance.
System responsibilities (not an actor) | Enforce access controls; route work to required actors; maintain required statuses/history; issue notifications/reminders. The system under specification is not modeled as a UC actor.
